# Succubus System

A dark fantasy demon progression system featuring traits, buildings, armies, interactions, and dynamic events centered around demonic evolution.

## Overview

The **Succubus System** introduces a unique demon-based gameplay mechanic where characters can evolve through multiple demonic forms, unlock powerful abilities, and eventually achieve true immortality.

The system is built around:

* Demon evolution traits
* Character interactions
* Absorption mechanics
* Trait progression tracks
* Special events and decisions
* Demonic armies and infrastructure

---

# Demon Forms

There are four primary demon forms in the system:

| Form            | Gender | Tier | Description                   |
| --------------- | ------ | ---- | ----------------------------- |
| Lesser Succubus | Female | Low  | Entry-level female demon form |
| Succubus        | Female | Mid  | Advanced female demon form    |
| Lesser Demon    | Male   | Low  | Entry-level male demon form   |
| Demon           | Both   | High | Final ascended demon form     |

## Final Form: Demon

The **Demon** form is the ultimate evolution state.

Requirements:

* Can only evolve from:

  * `Succubus`
  * `Lesser Demon`
* Must complete the full trait progression track
* Additional hidden conditions may apply

Benefits:

* True immortality
* Superior demonic abilities
* Enhanced interactions and influence

---

# Becoming a Demon

There are two primary ways to acquire demon traits.

## 1. Direct Interaction

Characters may gain demon traits by interacting with existing demons.

Possible requirements include:

* Sharing the same religion
* Sharing the same culture
* Being related to the demon
* Having a strong positive relationship
* Holding a powerful title:

  * King
  * Emperor
  * Other high-ranking rulers

## 2. Female Event Path

Female characters may become demons through special events if they:

* Already possess the demon trait
* Know the demon secret
* Gain the trait through scripted events

# Trait Progression System

Each demon form contains a progression track powered by experience points.

As the track advances:

* Lifespan increases
* Demonic abilities improve
* New interactions become available
* Evolution becomes possible

---

# Absorption Mechanic

All demons possess a unique interaction called:

## Absorption

Absorption allows demons to drain vitality from other characters to gain progression experience.

### Lesser Demon Absorption

Male demons (`Lesser Demon`) can absorb any character directly.

Effects on victims:

* Reduced lifespan
* Reduced health

### Female Demon Absorption

Female demon forms require special conditions for absorption.

Requirements:

* Targets must possess compatible `"cum"` modifications
* Usually obtained through prostitution-related mechanics

---

# Lifespan & Immortality

Demons are **not naturally immortal**.

Base demon forms:

* Can still die from old age
* Gain additional lifespan through trait progression

Only the final evolution:

## Demon

grants:

* True immortality
* Freedom from aging death
